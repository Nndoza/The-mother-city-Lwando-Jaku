The Mother City


The lovely city of Cape Town

Having spent most of my life in Johannesburg, I finally made the move to Cape Town in 2025. Cape Town has always been one of my favourite cities because of its breathtaking scenery, stunning landscapes, and beautiful ocean views.

Things I Enjoy Doing in Cape Town

  1. Exploring different restaurants and trying a variety of foods.
  2. Visiting Table Mountain, which is always an unforgettable experience.
  3. Watching the sunset at the V&A Waterfront while enjoying the atmosphere and views.

When I first moved to Cape Town, I lived in the city centre. While I enjoyed being close to everything, I found the area a little too busy for my lifestyle. I later moved slightly outside the city, where I can enjoy more nature while still having access to the incredible views that make Cape Town so special.

Things I still want to do

  • Skydiving – I would love to experience the thrill of jumping from a plane and seeing Cape Town's stunning coastline, mountains, and city views from above.
  • Shark Cage Diving – One of the most exciting experiences I hope to try is shark cage diving. It would be an unforgettable opportunity to see these incredible creatures up close while exploring another side of Cape Town's marine life.

During the summer, I spend a lot of time at the beach, taking advantage of the warm weather and coastal lifestyle. Living in Cape Town has been an amazing experience, and I continue to appreciate everything the Mother City has to offer.
